Questions & Answers

How do I place an order?

Tap the bag icon on any product — WhatsApp opens with the product name already written. Send it with your delivery date and address and we confirm the details with you.

Do you deliver the same day?

Yes, for most ready designs when you order early in the day. Custom boxes and large giveaway orders need more time — message us and we'll confirm.

Can I customise the box or add a name?

Absolutely. We personalise toppers, chocolate prints, cards and ribbon colours for birthdays, weddings, corporate gifts and national occasions.

Do you take bulk orders for events?

Yes — our Giveaways collection is made for weddings, baby showers, schools and companies. Share the quantity and date and we'll send you options.

How should I store the chocolate?

Keep it in a cool place away from direct sun (around 16–20°C). Flowers last longest with fresh water and away from AC drafts.
